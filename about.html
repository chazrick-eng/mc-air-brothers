Our Story

A local team you can count on

MC Air Brothers Ltd is a brothers-run cooling and energy company based right here in Discovery Bay, St. Ann. We help our neighbours across the parish stay cool, get reliable hot water, keep steady water pressure, and harness solar energy to cut their bills.

From a single wall unit in a family home to solar and cooling systems for a busy commercial space, we handle the sales, installation and ongoing service ourselves — so there's one trusted local team behind the whole job.
